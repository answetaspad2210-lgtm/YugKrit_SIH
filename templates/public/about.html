About YugKrit

YugKrit — "CONNECTING INDIA'S CHALLENGES WITH INDIA'S POTENTIAL" — is a societal innovation and collaboration platform that connects Government bodies, Universities, ULBs/NGOs, and Students to solve real-world civic problems through verified, tracked, and measurable student-led solutions.

Why YugKrit?

Millions of genuine local problems — from park safety to waste management — never reach the people capable of solving them. Meanwhile thousands of talented student teams look for real, meaningful projects. YugKrit closes that gap with a transparent, verified pipeline: from problem submission, to government verification, to university-led project execution, to community-validated impact and permanent student recognition.

Built for scale

YugKrit's architecture is intentionally modular. Government, University, ULB/NGO and Student dashboards share one authentication system, one database, and one permission model — making it straightforward to add Industry, Citizen, Mentor, or other future dashboards without rebuilding the platform.

Verified

Every challenge and solution is checked by government and community stakeholders.

Modular

Roles, permissions and dashboards are all data-driven and extensible.

Measurable

Impact, skills, and achievements are tracked automatically end-to-end.
