How YugKrit Works

A transparent lifecycle from real-world problem to verified, certified solution.

1. Problem Submission

A ULB or NGO submits a real societal problem with location, evidence, and affected population data.

2. AI Analysis & Government Verification

The problem is auto-categorized and prioritized by YugKrit's AI engine, then reviewed and verified by a government officer.

3. University Assignment

Government assigns the verified challenge to a matched university, or universities apply from the open marketplace.

4. Project & Team Formation

The university creates a project and builds a student team — each student linked uniquely by institution + registration number, so no duplicate profiles are ever created.

5. Milestones: Research → Design → Prototype → Testing → Validation

The team executes through tracked milestones, submitting deliverables for faculty review at each stage.

6. Community Validation & Verification

The ULB/NGO and government verify the delivered solution and its real-world impact.

7. Achievements, Certificates & Growth Profile

On verified completion, achievements and certificates are generated automatically, and every student's permanent Innovation Growth Profile is updated.
